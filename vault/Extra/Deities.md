[[Ugracaṇḍā (उग्रचण्डा)]]
> Kali manifests in three main cosmic (vyasti) forms, [[Mahākālī (महाकाली)]], [[Mahālakṣmi (महालक्ष्मी)]] and [[Mahāsarasvatī (महासरस्वती)]]. Representing the three gunas tamas (inertia), rajas (dynamism) and sattvas (luminosity) respectively. In the phenomenal universe, they underlie all the subsequent activities of creation, sustenance, and dissolution.

[[Kubjikā (कुब्जिका)]] 
> The Tantric deity Kubjikā ("the hunchbacked one" in Sanskrit) stands as one of the lesser-known goddesses within Hinduism, associated with the non-Siddhāntika Kubjikāmata sect.

[[Pratyaṅgirā (प्रत्यङ्गिरा)]]
> The pure manifestation of the wrath of [[Tripurāsundarī (त्रिपुरासुन्दरी)]], the goddess connected to the concept of the three cities as well as Sri Vidya.

[[Cāmuṇḍā (चामुण्डा)]]
