The three fundamental qualities or constituents of nature (_prakṛti_) in Sāṅkhya, Yoga, and many Vedāntic and Tantric systems
- **Sattva** (सत्त्व) 
- **Rajas** (रजस्) 
- **Tamas** (तमस्)

Without the unbalance of the gunas there wouldn’t be any manifest matter in [[Prakṛti (प्रकृति)]]. Equally if you balance your own gunas, you reach a higher unmanifest state. Spattika malas focus only on sattva, whereas rudraksh malas focus on all three gunas.

Each guna is divided, each guna having three within, making 9. 9 navadurgas. Then again, each [[Devīmahātmyam (देवीमहात्म्य)]] kalpa has its own set of 9 durgas.
