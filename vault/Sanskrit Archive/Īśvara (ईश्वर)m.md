Jñana (knowledge). Idam-aham; This-ness in I-ness; “this universe is my expansion”. Also Śiva in anthropomorphised form. 

See [[Svara (स्वर)]], under "Ī": 
**Ī** (ई) is Amritadrava who is identical to the principle of Īśvara. Who pacifies all forms of heat (or all suffering), who is the counter-image of the absolute (A) and who is resting on the state of the blossoming of boundless volition, and who wears the moon or the rays of nectar as his crest jewel.
