The Kāraṇaśarīra or causal body is Samaṣṭyajñānopahitacaitanya, the collective consciousness having the totality of ignorance. It is called [[Śiva (शिव)|Īśvara]], who is omniscient and omnipotent. Being the cause of the world, this divine totality is called as the Kāraṇaśarīra. Pratyabhijñā also accepts Īśvara as the cause for the outer shining of the world, without calling him Kāraṇaśarīra.
