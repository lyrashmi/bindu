center as aposed to [[Bhūpura (भूम्पुर)]]

The point of unity or ultimate reality

Represents the advaita, whereas bhupura represents advaita. Both together are [[Paramadvaita (परमद्वैत)]] or [[Mahārtha (महार्थ)]]

Bindu is the subject. Or the autonomous body in the act of cognition. Recognises inwardness, at the same time has the zeal to externalise in the most extreme form of materiality. [[Visarga (विसर्ग)]] 

Bindu has Gauri [[Śakti (शक्ति)]], [[Nāda (नाद)]] has Jheshta emanating, vāma from [[Bīja (बीज)]]. These three aspects are visualised as emanating from the triadic bindu nada bija. These three give rise to rudra, rama and vishnu. So this is the very triadic structure of the rishis brahma vishnu rudra. 
Iccha jnana kriya, depicting agni, surya and new moon. There arises the rava from the nada, and that is what is called the shambhda brahman.
