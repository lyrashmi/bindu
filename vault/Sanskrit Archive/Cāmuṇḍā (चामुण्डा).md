One of the Yoginis, she is a wrathful emanation of Caṇḍika (see: [[Ugracaṇḍā (उग्रचण्डा)]]) and represents her power or [[Śakti (शक्ति)|śakti]], she is therefore [[Krama (क्रम)|anākhya]]. She is also one of the [[Aśtamātṝkā (अष्टमातृका)]].

Named after Chanda and Munda, after killing them in the [[Devīmahātmyam (देवीमहात्म्य)]].
Chanda and Munda (lit. head) are the rage, the anger; the one’s who would kill because they are angry.
