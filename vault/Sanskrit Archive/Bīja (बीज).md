A seed syllable mantra, doesn’t hold linguistic meaning beyond its primary function; it’s fundamental vibration ([[Spanda (स्पन्द)]]) making up the body of a deity. A bīja is the essential sound of a primary cause or principle from which something is produced.

As a syllable of concentrated power with its own distinct vibration, a bija is the essential component of any mantra.

[[Bindu (बिन्दु)]] has Gauri [[Śakti (शक्ति)]], [[Nāda (नाद)]] has Jheshta emanating, vāma from Bija. These three aspects are visualised as emanating from the triadic bindu nada bija. These three give rise to rudra, rama and vishnu. So this is the very triadic structure of the rishis brahma vishnu rudra. 
Iccha jnana kriya, depicting agni, surya and new moon. There arises the rava from the nada, and that is what is called the shambhda brahman.
