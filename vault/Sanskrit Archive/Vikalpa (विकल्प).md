Thought, false thought constructs.

Neither reject anything, nor accept, abide in your essential Self which is an Eternal presence.  
Abhinavagupta says: “When there is vikalpa, neither accept. nor reject, it will retire of itself and you will find yourself to be what you are."  
This is an artless art. It is effortless. spontaneous.  
This Sambhava [[Upāya (उपाय)]] is, in one sense, the simplest, in another, the most difficult.

Nirvikalpa Chaitanya (Undifferentiated Consciousness) - that consciousness which is without any distinctions or conceptualisation.
