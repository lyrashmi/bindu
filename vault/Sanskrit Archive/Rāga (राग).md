lit. to colour, to dye

Refers to passion or attraction

[[Kāmeśvarī (कामेश्वरी)]] (the goddess of desire) is raga.

seen as a counterpart to [[Dveṣa (द्वेष)]]

You cannot ever expunge the sensory faculties ([[Indra (इन्द्र)]]) of the need or want to consume.
