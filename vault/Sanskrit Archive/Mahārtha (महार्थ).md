lit. Great reality. Both the immanent and trancendent (see: [[Krama (क्रम)]], [[Spanda (स्पन्द)]]). Absolute all inclusive non-dualism. Identical to [[Paramadvaita (परमद्वैत)]]. Identical to [[Ugracaṇḍā (उग्रचण्डा)]]. You have the Maharthamanjari by Maheshvarananda as well as the Maharthamajaripraimalah, a self-authored commentary on that text.

The heart of Sarvamnaya is **Mahartha**, the heart of Mahartha is [[Svātantrya (स्वातन्त्र्य)]]. Think of advaita, or Kalika as [[Bindu (बिन्दु)]] and carvaka as [Bhūpura (भूम्पुर)](Bhūpura%20(भूम्पुर).md). only that, our Advaita is Mahartha, the absolute monism (for the lack of a better term) of the Kali pantheon.

Mahartha’s three main aspects of knowledge:
1. Pramāṇa (प्रमाण) – The means or sources of knowledge. I.e. perception, inference, testimony, atma-vichara etc.
2. Prameya (प्रमेय) – The objects of knowledge. I.e. the things or concepts that are the subject of knowledge. I.e. the nature of the Self.
3. Puruṣārtha (पुरुषार्थ) – The goal or purpose of knowledge. I.e. the recognition of the Self not as limited (anu), but as the universal divine source from which all things spring and return to.
(You also have pramata, the subject)
