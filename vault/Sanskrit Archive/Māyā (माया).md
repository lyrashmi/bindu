lit. ‘Deceit, cosmic illusion’. 
A veil where things appear one way but are not the way they seem. The five [[Kañcuka (कञ्चुक)]]'s (limiting factors on physical reality) are what comprise it. Māyā is derived from ‘Ma’, “to measure out”; It is what makes everyday experience measurable.

Mahamāyā, or great illusion is a deified form of this power. The Devī ([[Ugracaṇḍā (उग्रचण्डा)]]) doesn't only create the universe, she also actively hides herself making it difficult for the limited creature ([[Jīva (जीव)]]) to know their true divine nature.
