Life force, vitality.

Before we eat, offer the 5 first bites to the pranas:
1. For Prana: Om Prānāya Swāhā
 (Offering to Prana, the upward-moving vital force.)
2. For Apana: Om Apānāya Swāhā
(Offering to Apana, the downward-moving vital force.)
3. For Vyana: Om Vyānāya Swaha
(Offering to Vyana, the all-pervading vital force.)
4. For Udana: Om Udānāya Swāhā
(Offering to Udana, the upward-projecting vital force.)
5. For Samana: Om Samanāya Swāhā
(Offering to Samana, the vital force of digestion and assimilation.)

This practice is a [[Yajña (यज्ञ)]] into the inner fire

Once the Yogī exploits the units of time while controlling prāṇa and apāna, he reaches to the level in which his karmic merits and demerits burn. His kālādhva (path of time) refines and he unites with Śakti and settles in the Sahasrāra. [[Śiva (शिव)|Bhairava]] quivers as a tattva in real state, in front of the Yogī."
- Jayaratha, commentary on Tantrāloka I.99

In the Muladhara the goddess is worshipped in the form of providing foundation ([[Ādhāra (आधार)]]), the body; and in the Svadishtaana she is worshipped as fluidity in the form of life force of vitality (prana).

- Prāṇa pratiṣṭhā (प्राणप्रतिष्ठा) - lit. ‘Life force, breath’ & ‘Resting, position; or installed’. It is a rite or ceremony by which a murti is consecrated, infusing life (prana) into it.
