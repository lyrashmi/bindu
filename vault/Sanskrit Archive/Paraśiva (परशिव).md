Paraśiva is the highest aspect of [[Śiva (शिव)]] identified by the first five [[Tattva (कन्द)]]'s.

- Śiva (शिव) - Consciousness (Cit). Aham (I am); pure state of transcendental I-ness. Who’s manifest state is prakaśa (light). 
- Śakti (शक्ति) - Power of consciousness (Citi). Ānanda (bliss). Aham (I am); Pure state of immanent I-ness. Who’s manifest state is vimarśa (the power of the I-ness to reflect upon itself).
- Sadāśiva (सदाशिव) - Icchā (will). Aham-idam; I-ness in this-ness “I am this universe”.
- Īśvara (ईश्वर) - Jñana (knowledge). Idam-aham; This-ness in I-ness; “this universe is my expansion”. Also Śiva in anthropomorphised form. 
* Śuddhavidyā (शुद्धविद्या) - Kriyā (action). Aham-aham-idam-idam; I-ness and this-ness separated (duality).
