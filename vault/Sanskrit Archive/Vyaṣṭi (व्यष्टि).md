lit. ‘Seperate’. Individuality, singleness. Signifies the state of an ingredient. Vyaṣṭi is contradistinguished from [[Samaṣṭi (समष्टि)]] which signifies the state of the compound. 

Used to talk about the universal energies; inertia, dynamism and dissolution. In the phenomenal universe, they underlie all the subsequent activities of creation, sustenance, and dissolution.
