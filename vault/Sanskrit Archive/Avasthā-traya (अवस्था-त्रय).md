_The three states of consciousness_.

These are:
1. **Jāgrat** (जाग्रत्) — waking
2. **Svapna** (स्वप्न) — dreaming
3. **Suṣupti** (सुषुप्ति) — deep sleep (or **nidrā** in a general sense)

Then there is **turiya**, the fourth state transcending these.

When you have no knowledge of object-consciousness that is nidra. When you have knowledge of things that are not there that is svapna.
