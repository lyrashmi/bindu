Pure consciousness (shuddha caitanya), equalled to [[Prakāśa (प्रकाश)]]. See also: [[Akula (अकुल)]].

The mere perception of the colour blue, free from all secondary cognitive processes corresponds to the principle known as [[Sadāśiva (सदाशिव)|Sadaśiva]] (see also: [[Pañcapretāḥ (पञ्चप्रेताः)]]). The Rudras are below this state, but they are not under maya. Being a Rudra means there being difference (not a complete dissolution of difference), yet still being submerged in Śiva state. They are in the stage of śuddha [[Vidyā (विद्या)|vidya]], but not in the nirvikalpa (no [[Vikalpa (विकल्प)]]) state of singularity. Therefore they come in the form of [[Vāk (वाक्)|mantra]], (see also: [[Mārga (मार्ग)]]).

Shiva is [[Samaṣṭi (समष्टि)|samasthi]] caitanya. Sat (the absolute) and Tat (the manifest) form satya (truth), there is nothing outside of Shiva.

When Satī dissolves in Shiva, this is when you call him Shambhu. Related to the six Shambhava wisdoms from the [[Āgamas (आगम)]]. The world (jagad) also pulsates in the heart of Shiva in the form of Satī. 

His five faces correspond to the [[Āmnāya (आम्नाय)|amnayas]].

Highest state is Svacchanda Bhairava.
