Manu in Hindu cosmology:
Fourteen Manus: Hindu cosmology divides time into vast cycles called Manvantaras, each lasting 306.72 million years.
• Each Manvantara is presided over by a specific Manu, making him the ruler and progenitor of that era.
• The current Manvantara is the seventh, governed by Vaivasvata Manu, a descendant of the sun god (Surya)
