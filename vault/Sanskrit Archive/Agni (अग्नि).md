Fire & deity of same name.

Since fire contains both pure consciousness, vac spanda/ahata nada ([[Nāda (नाद)]]) and unmanifest vac/anahata nada, it is the most direct carrier between the elements to the gods. And hence the goddess herself is the fire.

Svaha is the agni devata, the consort of fire, the shakti within fire. Svada, she is the one who takes the offerings to gods and [[Pitṛs (पितृस्)]] and brings it to them. Also vashat, pat, hoom.
When she’s the vowels (svara) she’s also the consonants, because the consonants originate from the vowels.
When she is invoked as the vowels, she is the nityas (15 goddesses as aspects of the moon with the secret 16th one usually being tripura sundari). 16 vowels. Both of the character of vakshat and all the aksharas (she who does not do decay)
You are the sandhya (lit. transition, junction) you are the dawn and dusk

Kali is the power of fire who caries the oblation to the gods, and ancestral spirits.

Fire is the collective of the gods, fire is through which all of the gods receive the food. If there’s no food offered through the Fire, no God receives any food. The fire doesn’t eat by mere dumping, the gods eat through Svaha. The goddess has uttara and dakshina (north and south) margas (channels) for the deva yajna (where she manifests in the form of Svaha) and the pittri yajna (where she manifests in the form of Svada).
