Just like a metal, if you don't polish it, it won't reflect any light (which doesn't mean that the light isn't going there) - therefore when the surface of buddhi (the intellect) is refined, then the external world mirrors itself there, and it becomes vidya. If it isn't refined it remains avidya. Make your buddhi a candidate for reflecting the external world, so that it becomes vidya. Reflection both in terms of reflecting outward, but also as in to think - both are reflection. Our intellect is a conduit for the light to pass through.  

The visualisations occur pre-intellect - but we humans are simply doomed to intellectualise. Seeing through your intellect will be like watching the ray of light shining through your bedroom window, not actually going and experiencing what the sun itself looks like. We are manu; born with manus (mind) - we are intellectual creatures. This is our blessing - though sometimes it is not a blessing; we over-intellectualise and miss the bigger picture (like thinking that the ray through the window is the totality of the sun).
