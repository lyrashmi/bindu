Abhāsa - Shining forth ; Pratibijna - Reflecting that shining forth.

[[Prakāśa (प्रकाश)]] and [[Vimarśa (विमर्श)]]

Consciousness is the perfect surface for reflection due to being vimala (without [[Mala (मल)]]).

The ultimate Principle of Reality is Consciousness reposing in Her own innate fullness, endowed eternally with the powers of knowing, willing and doing in whom the entire universe consisting of 36 principles (tattvas) is reflected non-differently like a city in the mirror. This ultimate principle can be called Kālī or simply, Anākhya (the Nameless/ inexplicable of the [[Krama (क्रम)|kramas]], containing all three in unison).
