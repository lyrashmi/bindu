The 64 Yoginis are behind the principle of 5-fold [[Mahārtha (महार्थ)]] worship known as the Vrndacakra (the wheel of totality) in the Kalikula Kalika [[Krama (क्रम)]] system, and are acting as an extension of the system of 12 Kalis as talked about extensively by Abhinavagupta. The Kramasadbhāva extends these 12 to 17 (the 17th again being the unity of all) and the rest of the 16 each having 4 Yoginis, making the total of 64. 

**The fivefold Yogini worship, in Mahārtha philosophy functions like this:** 
* First comes the wheel of the 16 Jnanasiddhas and their respective Yoginis.
* Then the wheel of 24 Mantrasiddhas and their respective Yoginis. 
	- At this stage traces of the twelve constituents of the cognitive wheel of light ([[Prakāśa (प्रकाश)|prakáśa]]-cakra) are obliterated. Here 24 Yognis are worshipped, because each of the twelve has a latent and an active aspect
* Then of the wheel of 12 Melāpasiddhas and their respective Yoginis.
* Then the wheel of 8 Śaktasiddhas and their respective Yoginis.
* Then the wheel of 4  Śāmbhavasiddhas and their respective Yoginis.

These add up to 64.
