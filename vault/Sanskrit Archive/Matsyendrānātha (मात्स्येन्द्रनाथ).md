Matsyendranatha is the founder of the kaula ([[Kula (कुल)]]) system in the Kali yuga 

**The triangle, the four foundational Peethas of tantric practice**
Kapujau (acronym of the first syllables), Kamarupa, Purnagiri, Jalandhara, Oddiyana.
**And their Yoganathas (Lords of the ages)**
Kakumema (again an acronym of all four), Matsyendranatha (Lord of the Fish, Kamarupa Peetha, Kali Yuga), Meshanatha (Lord of the Ram, Purnagiri Peetha, Dvapara Yuga), Kurmanatha (The Lord of the Turtoise, Jalandhara Peetha, Treta Yuga), Khagendranatha (Lord of the Birds, Oddiyana Peetha, Satya Yuga).
