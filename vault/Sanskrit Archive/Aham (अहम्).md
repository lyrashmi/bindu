Aham: A (the absolute) referring to Shiva, Ha (the rays of the absolute) referring to Shakti and Ma (anu) referring to my individuated state of being. Tripura is this triad. [[Visarga (विसर्ग)]] arises from the inner potent union of shiva and shakti (samayachara, [[Samarasya (समरस्य)]]). The double dots : where one is A (the absolute) and the other is Ham (the emission) - aham.

Backwards spelling of: [[Maha (मह)]]
