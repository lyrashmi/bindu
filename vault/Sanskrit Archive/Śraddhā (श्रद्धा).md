This śraddhā is not just simply faith. First, you inquire, first you explore. And then when you test whether it is following śruti, you just see whether this is in accordance with śruti or not, then you go with other pramāṇa-s, whether this is something blindly given or is there some type of validity in what is being said. And when you test it, it is śruti pramāṇa siddha, it is pratyakṣa anumāna siddha, that it is established from the pramāṇa of the words, the testimony, or it is established by direct apprehension, or inference, then you do not keep checking every day. Do you just go to the goldsmith every day and ask him to check if what you are wearing is gold or not? You firmly believe it is. The same way, when you first test something to see whether it is true, do not embrace something without testing, and once you test it, do not make a habit of going to the goldsmith everyday, asking “Can you test this for me, please?” Because it is just annoying, to say the least.
