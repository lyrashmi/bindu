means of knowledge, valid source of knowledge

**perception (pratyakṣa)**, **inference (anumāna)**, and **testimony (śabda)**.

Apauruṣeya Pramāṇa literally translates to "authorless valid knowledge" or "impersonal source of knowledge." In practice, this term is used almost exclusively to refer to the Vedas. And is used particularly within the Mīmāṃsā and Vedānta schools.
