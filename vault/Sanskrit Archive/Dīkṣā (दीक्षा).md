 Initiation rites. In Sarvāmnāya there are four levels of initiation: Samaya, putraka, sādhaka, ācārya. Each level of dīkṣā bestows certain rights and responsibilities on the initiate and increases complexity of sādhana (spiritual practice).

Texts in Nepal I've seen speak only of three Pāśupata dīkṣa-s: Laghu, Madhyama, Mahā. I understand that they emanate, respectively, a fourfold, fivefold and sixfold Āmnāya system

The fourfold following a Trika structure (3+1), the fivefold a Mahārtha structure (sṛṣṭi, sthiti, saṁhāra, anākhya, bhāsa), the sixfold a Kaubjikā structure
