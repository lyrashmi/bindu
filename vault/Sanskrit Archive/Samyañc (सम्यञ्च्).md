Proper, accurate, moving in harmony.

From the prefix _sam_ (together, completely) combined with the root _añc_ (to bend, move, or direct).

In Buddhism, it is the foundational qualifier for the Noble Eightfold Path, where it is often translated as right or perfect. This can be seen in terms like _samyag-dṛṣṭi_ (Right View) or _samyak-smṛti_ (Right Mindfulness).

In Vedānta _samyag-jñāna_ refers to the absolute, unblemished knowledge of the ultimate reality.

Opposed to [[Moha (मोह)]].
