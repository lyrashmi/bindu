The pashupati lingam's fifth upwards face is always formless (nirguna), and of absolute prakaśa/vimarśa only. The dialogue of the amnayas: Dialogue is an act of recognition. Recognising in the world, feeling what others are feeling, dissolving boundaries.

This very expression becomes your intrinsic nature (svabhava). Let's meditate on this expression of the intrinsic nature in the manifold of Shakti.
