Pralaya refers to a period of cosmic dissolution when the universe is destroyed and then re-created. 
1. Nitya Pralaya (Daily Dissolution)
Happens at the end of every day for living beings, symbolizing the cycle of birth and death.
2. Naimittika Pralaya (Occasional Dissolution)
Occurs at the end of a Kalpa (one day of Brahma, approximately 4.32 billion years). During this time, the material world is dissolved temporarily, but higher realms remain intact.
3. Prakritika Pralaya (Natural Dissolution)
Happens at the end of Brahma’s lifespan (100 years of Brahma, approximately 311 trillion human years). At this time, the entire universe merges into the primal matter (Prakriti), and even time ceases to exist.
4. Atyantika Pralaya (Eternal Dissolution)
Refers to liberation (moksha), when an individual soul is freed from the cycle of birth and rebirth.
