lit. ‘Veil, coat’. 
Five of the [[Tattva (कन्द)]]'s. The limiting factors of reality, which Śiva has to take on in order to manifest inside of the [[Jīva (जीव)]]. According to the Trika system, these are fivefold:

- Niyati tattva - (limitation on space) 
- Kāla tattva - (limitation on time)
- [[Rāga (राग)]] tattva - (attachment; feeling of incompleteness). Though you could say that the exception to this is attachment towards pure knowledge ([[Mahāvidyā (महाविद्या)]]), which instead is grace ([[Svātantrya (स्वातन्त्र्य)]])
- Vidyā tattva - (limited knowledge) 
- [[Kalā (कला)]] tattva - (limited creativity). This tattva produces the five elements, not [[Māyā (माया)]].
