The self-shining light of awareness. The transcendent concept of the absolute. [[Śiva (शिव)|Śiva]]’s manifest state. [[Akula (अकुल)]]

See also: [[Vimarśa (विमर्श)]], [[Abhāsa-Pratibimba (अभास-प्रतिबिम्ब)]], [[Tripurāsundarī (त्रिपुरासुन्दरी)]]

The absolute harmony is the absolute harmony of prakasha-vimarsha, the illuminating form of consciousness with its reflexivity, with its self-affirmation; my awareness affirming itself, affirming its being, affirming its presence; me totally being aware of my awareness - and this is what samayachara is.
