Karma is a continuity in the mind and the body - tendencies, habits and behaviour patterns which are triggered by todays actions. Hence karma is two-fold. In the shakta world karma is an expression, a distilled materialisation of jnana shakti -> and that jnana shakti is a distilled form of iccha shakti. And therefore there is no dichotomy between jnana and karma; it is one and the same. So by means of our external actions we can actually actualise/ recognise our true selfs - because there is no dichotomy between inside and outside; there is no dichotomy between matter and mind; there is no dichotomy between conciousness and the body - and in that sense there is no dichotomy between jnana and karma, it is one and the same. It is a continuity and we need to recognise that.
