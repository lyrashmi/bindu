lit. She who devours time

The posture of Kāla Saṃkarṣiṇī seated on [[Śiva (शिव)|Manthana Bhairava]]’s lap symbolizes the dominance of the timeless void (akāla) over the play of time (kāla). While Bhairava churns the ocean of samsāra (samudra manthana) to extract essence (amrita), Kāla Saṃkarṣiṇī absorbs the entire process into herself, signifying the end of all cycles.

The arduous penance here signifying the stirring, or churning by manthanabhairava (where manthana translates to “to churn”), of the ocean of samsara in order to extract essence (amrita), by means of tapas.

The again, according to Trika: “When she engenders the universe by imprinting life on it, she is called Kāli and when she reabsorbs it into herself, Kālasamkarśani, 'she who squeezes Time', to empty it of its content.” (see also: [[Krama (क्रम)]])
