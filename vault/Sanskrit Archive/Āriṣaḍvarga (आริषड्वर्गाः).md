The six internal enemies, known as the arishadvargas or shadripus. These are: kāma (desire/lust), krodha (anger), lobha (greed), mada (ego), moha (attachment), and mātsarya (jealousy). They are viewed as negative traits that hinder spiritual progress and the attainment of moksha (liberation). 

The Bhagavad Gita discusses kāma and krodha extensively.
