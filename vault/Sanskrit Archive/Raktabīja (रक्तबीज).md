Raktabija, the representative demonic blood drop bija which has the power of infinite multiplication, which Bhadrakālī/ Chamunda sucks dry in the [[Devīmahātmyam (देवीमहात्म्य)]].

The Rakta[[Bīja (बीज)]] is the bija that "I am this jiva", fueling the false ego-sense ([[Ahaṃkāra (अहंकार)]])

The Bhaava that “I am Jiva” is the Rakthabija. The jiva bhava or the Conditioned Individual Ego is the Rakthabija. From the subtle see of this Ego, again manifests the gross form of Ego – the Jivatva or Dvaita Bhava. [[Mahiṣāsuramardinī (महिषासुरमर्दिनी)]] in his bull form is the great ego.

By Chamunda drinking Raktabijas blood it signifies the obstruction of the cycle of identification (jiva bhava) and liberation into the unconditioned state of being (shuddha chaitanya).
