lit. _fault_, _defect_, or _blemish_

Doshas in ayurveda are threefold:
1. Vata (Air and Ether)
2. Pitta (Fire and Water)
3. Kapha (Water and Earth)
A persons unique balance of these are their *prakriti*, imbalance in one or more doshas is called *vikriti*

___
The doshas of the three [[Guṇa (गुण)]] (hence it is important to balance them):
**Sattva**
- **Excessive attachment to purity or goodness**: Leads to self-righteousness, pride, or spiritual arrogance.
- **Complacency**: A person may become overly passive, avoiding necessary actions for growth.
- **Detachment from reality**: Excessive focus on ideals may cause a disconnect from practical life

**Rajas**
- **Desires and attachments**: Leads to constant craving, dissatisfaction, and over ambition.
- **Restlessness**: Causes agitation, lack of focus, and burnout.
- **Self-centredness**: Rajas-driven individuals may prioritise personal gain over others, fostering greed or jealousy

**Tamas**
- **Ignorance**: Leads to lack of awareness, delusion, or confusion.
- **Lethargy**: Promotes procrastination, stagnation, and lack of motivation.
- **Destructive tendencies**: Can manifest as violence, depression, or harmful actions toward self or others
