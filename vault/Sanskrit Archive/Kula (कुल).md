Kula, group. [[Jagad (जगत्)]], [[Cāmuṇḍā (चामुण्डा)]], [[Vimarśa (विमर्श)]]

In the technical sense found in tantric sources, Kula primarily denotes the sphere of immanence, variously understood, contrasted with the transcendent [[Akula (अकुल)]] (not Kula), with which it forms a pair, Kaula.

Kula below and Akula above encapsulate [[Śiva (शिव)|Sadāśiva]].
