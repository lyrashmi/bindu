Unmeṣa is the inclination towards manifestation, revelation being the essential nature of the divine. More literally, opening out. For example, The world is an unmesa, or expansion (prasura) of the supreme as [[Śakti (शक्ति)|śakti]]. A distinct blossoming of the universe, enabled by the closing of the eyes of [[Śiva (शिव)]].

Nimeṣa is the opposite, the opening of the eyes of Śiva leading to the dissolution of the world.
