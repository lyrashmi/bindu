The three means (upāyas) of recognising the absolute. Which include: 
* **Aṇavopāya**, the means of action (visualisation, pranayama, ritual) 
* **Śāktopāya**, the purification of mind and sharpening of the intellect trough the processes of consciousness, by refining thought in succession 
* **Śāmbhavopāya**, the means of pure will to recognise our true nature (svabhāva).

How to channel divine will into a specific direction. We cultivate will (iccha) with Shambhavaupaya (way of shiva), With Shaktaupaya we cultivate our knowledge (through this we see how the world is different and again the same). 
Anavopaya is the way of the individual. Anupaya (no means, spontaneous realisation).
