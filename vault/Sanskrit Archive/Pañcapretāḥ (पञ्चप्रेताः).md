The Panchapretas (refers to “five ghosts”, according to the second recension of the Yogakhaṇḍa of the [[Kubjikā (कुब्जिका)|Manthānabhairavatantra]]) are the Pañcakaraṇa (group of five instruments (also called the five Brahmās), also called Karaṇapañcaka; which are the five deities of the faces of **[[Śiva (शिव)|Sadaśiva]]**, with Ishana the totality of the previous four - as defined by the Siddhāntāgamas), which constitute the five organs the eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin; the five functional organs speech, hands, feet, anus and sexual organs; the five internal organs, the thoughts, intellect, ego, mind, and knowledge (). In the **Guhyakālī** painting these are the five faces present in the topmost pedestal. The level below that is the [[Aśtamātṝkā (अष्टमातृका)|aśtamātṝkās]]. The level again once below that is the [[Daśadikpāla (दशदिक्पाल)|dasha dikpalakas]] (the 10 deities presiding over the different directions, 8 in the cardinal directions - then the upward direction and the secret downward direction).
