Path, way, or approach.

- **Kula Mārga** – the _path of the Kula_, esoteric Śākta-Śaiva Tantric tradition focused on the unity of Śiva and Śakti.
- **Mantra Mārga** – the _path of mantra_, involving ritual, deity worship, and initiatory practice.
- **Bhāva Mārga** – the _path of inner feeling or mood_, often referring to devotion or a specific spiritual disposition.
