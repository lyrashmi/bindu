While the Devīmāhātmyam (glory of the Goddess), also referred to as Durgā Saptaśatī (seven-hundred verses on Durgā), and Caṇḍī Pāṭha (recitation of Caṇḍī) is a section on the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, it also acts as the single most foundational text in Śakta tantrism today - and is generally considered to be one of the most well read and recited devotional texts in the Hindu world.

Mahavidyas are historically speaking a later development.  
Therefore the Devimahatmya stands as a collection of all the tantric vidyas.

The relationship of speech and meaning is avinabhava (one cannot exist without the other)
Therefore within the context of saptasathi or mantras in general, one should not look down upon simple utterance/recitation. It is as important as the deeper insight.
