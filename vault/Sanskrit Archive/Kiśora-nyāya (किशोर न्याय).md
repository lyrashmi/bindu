**1. Markata Kishora Nyaya**; _Monkey Baby Principle_
The Monkey Baby Principle compares devotees to baby monkeys, clinging tightly to God, remaining steadfast in their spiritual practice despite challenges.
**2. Maarjaala Kishora Nyaya**; _Kitten principle_
The Kitten Principle compares advanced devotees to kittens, trusting God to place them where they are meant to be and taking care of them.
