 lit. ‘Supreme knowlege; Great wisdom’. 
 Refers to a group of ten tantric goddesses; dāśa-mahāvidyās (dāśa meaning ten). In some śakta schools, these are all forms of [[Mahākālī (महाकाली)]]. The formation of this group encompass divergent and varied religious traditions that includes the worship of the [[Yoginī (योगिनी)]].
