All of the [[Svara (स्वर)]], (i.e. [[Bhairava (भैरव)|Bhairavas]]) culminate in visarga.

Emission; or absolute potential (visarga) is half of the letter H, which is the aggregate of the rays of both Kula and Akula (half of that again is [[Anuttara (अनुत्तर)]]). It is the desire of absolute consciousness to pour out of itself, as that is its nature. Hence emission (visarga) is the body (kula) of the nectar ([[Amṛta (अमृत)]]) of the absolute (anuttara).

[[Bindu (बिन्दु)]] is the subject. Or the autonomous body in the act of cognition. Recognises inwardness, at the same time has the zeal to externalise in the most extreme form of materiality (See also: [[Udyama (उद्यम)]]). Visarga (emission) is two drops: within that you have 1) ‘sishtrun-muhata-samharaun’ orientation towards dissolution, reaching towards singularity, reaching to order anuttara - and 2) orientation towards externalisation, all the way to shah, the dara, the prithvi tattva (earth). What is the visarga of [[Bindu (बिन्दु)]]? It is the agitation of vigor, i.e. prana expressing itself in the mode of joy. So what is the whole creation? It is the [[Bindu (बिन्दु)|bindu]]-vikshobda (the agitation of the seminal drop). This is where I am talking about surplus, the world is the surplus of joy.

It arises from the inner potent union of shiva and shakti (samayachara, [[Samarasya (समरस्य)]]). The double dots : where one is A (the absolute) and the other is Ham (the emission) - [[Aham (अहम्)]].
