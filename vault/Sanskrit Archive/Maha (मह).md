Maha - Ma (I, I am; aham), Ha ([[Vimarśa (विमर्श)]] shakti), A (the absolute, [[Prakāśa (प्रकाश)]]). We're misusing the word maha a lot, in reality: When we have the totality of the triad there, then that is maha.  [[Visarga (विसर्ग)]] arises from the inner potent union of shiva and shakti (samayachara, [[Samarasya (समरस्य)]]). The double dots : where one is A (the absolute) and the other is Ham (the emission) - [[Aham (अहम्)]].

[[Para (पर)]] indicates transcendence, whereas maha indicates immanence (that which is still replete with the triadic potencies, being under the domain of maya).
