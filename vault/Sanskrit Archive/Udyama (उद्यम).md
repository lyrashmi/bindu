Going upward. An initial throb inital exertion. This prathama: the rise, the first step of expression, which is replete within itself; within it there are no external or internal, there is no separation of aham and idam, i-am'ness and this'ness, the complete state of subject and object -  and because it is replete it is called by the name of Bhairava.  And within that there is also abhavāsana (manifestation which makes the rise of the externals possible).
